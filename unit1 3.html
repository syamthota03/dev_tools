PAWAN KALYAN

Pawan Kalyan is a famous Indian actor, politician, and social worker. He was born on September 2, 1971,in Andhra Pradesh.

Pawan Kalyan started his acting career with the movie Akkada Ammayi Ikkada Abbayi in 1996. He acted in many successful films such as Tholi Prema, Gabbar Singh, Attarintiki Daredi, and Vakeel Saab.

In addition to acting, Pawan Kalyan founded the Jana Sena Party in 2014. He encourages young people to participate in public service and bring positive change.
